# 📊 Mathematics for Data Science  

Mathematics is the foundation of Data Science, Machine Learning, and AI. This repository covers essential mathematical concepts required for understanding and implementing data science algorithms effectively.  

## 📌 Topics Covered  

### 🔹 **1. Linear Algebra** (For Machine Learning & Deep Learning)  
- Vectors & Matrices – Basis of data representation  
- Dot Product & Cross Product – Vector operations  
- Matrix Operations – Addition, multiplication, inverse, determinant  
- Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors – Used in PCA (Dimensionality Reduction)  
- Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) – Data compression & noise reduction  
- Tensors – Foundation of deep learning frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch)  

### 🔹 **2. Probability & Statistics** (For Data Analysis & ML Models)  
- Basic Probability – Conditional probability, Bayes' theorem  
- Probability Distributions – Normal, Poisson, Binomial, Exponential, Bernoulli  
- Expectation & Variance – Understanding spread & deviation  
- Hypothesis Testing – p-values, t-tests, chi-square tests  
- Central Limit Theorem (CLT) – Basis of statistical inference  
- Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) – Estimating parameters of distributions  

### 🔹 **3. Calculus** (For Optimization & ML Algorithms)  
- Differentiation – Used in Gradient Descent, finding minima/maxima  
- Partial Derivatives – Essential for multivariable functions  
- Gradient Descent Algorithm – Optimizing ML models  
- Chain Rule – Backpropagation in neural networks  
- Integration – Probability distributions & expectations  

### 🔹 **4. Optimization Techniques** (For Model Tuning & Training)  
- Convex Optimization – Finding optimal weights in ML models  
- Lagrange Multipliers – Optimization with constraints  
- Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) – Optimization in deep learning  
- Regularization Techniques – L1 (Lasso), L2 (Ridge), ElasticNet  

### 🔹 **5. Graph Theory** (For Network Analysis & Graph ML)  
- Graph Representations – Adjacency matrices, edge lists  
- Shortest Path Algorithms – Dijkstra’s, Bellman-Ford  
- PageRank Algorithm – Google search ranking  
- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) – Deep learning on graph structures  

### 🔹 **6. Information Theory** (For Feature Selection & NLP)  
- Entropy & Information Gain – Decision trees, feature importance  
- KL Divergence & Cross-Entropy Loss – Used in classification models  
- Shannon’s Theorem – Foundation of data encoding & compression
